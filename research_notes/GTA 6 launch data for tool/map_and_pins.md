# GTA VI Map (Leonida) and Map Pin Categories — research notes as of 2026-11-22

Confidence tags: **CONFIRMED** = official Rockstar, or several reputable post-launch guides agree. **LIKELY** = one reputable post-launch guide. **RUMOR/LEAK** = forums, pre-release speculation, fan maps, or unreliable or conflicting fan sites.
Dates are the publication dates of the sources, where visible. The game launched on 2026-11-19 ([Rockstar GTA VI page, "Now Available", Newswire post dated 2026-11-19](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI)). Most "guides" with dates of 2026-11-18 come from review or early-access play (for example, The Big Lead says it played for about 140 hours). So the data here is only 0–4 days old, and many totals are still unsettled.

**Source types used:**
- Official: Rockstar.
- Guide sites: PowerPyx, GTA Boss, GTABase, The Big Lead and others.
- Video: YouTube titles, descriptions and chapter timestamps, pulled with yt-dlp on 2026-11-22.
- Forum: GTAForums, known from search snippets only.

**Access limits on 2026-11-22:**
- Reddit (r/GTA6) returned HTTP 403 to both JSON and HTML requests. Web search surfaced no r/GTA6 threads, so I could not cite any Reddit posts or screenshots.
- The GTAForums mapping thread returned 403.

## 1. Regions and cities of the map

### Takeaway
Rockstar names six regions of the state of Leonida: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Rockstar has not published a size figure. Post-launch reports say the whole map is open from the start, but the story keeps you in the Leonida Keys for roughly the first 30–35% of the game. Unexplored areas are hidden under fog of war until you visit them.

### Cited Findings
- **Six official regions (CONFIRMED):** Vice City (the main city), Leonida Keys (islands), Grassrivers (mangrove wetlands), Port Gellhorn (a faded resort town), Ambrosia (the industrial and agricultural heartland) and Mount Kalaga National Park (forest along the northern border). — [GTA Boss map guide, 2026-11-15](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-map); [games.gg, 2026-11-18](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-map-unlock-how-to-reveal-full-map/); [MMO Culture](https://mmoculture.com/tips-guides/gta-6-map-guide/)
- **Rockstar's official page** shows Leonida as the state and Vice City as "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America". The visible page content gives no map size. — [Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) (CONFIRMED)
- **Official map download (LIKELY):** A printable poster and map of Leonida is a free digital download on the official GTA VI site from 2026-11-19. A double-sided physical map is listed at $0.99 on the Rockstar Store, or free with any Rockstar Store purchase. — [GTA Boss, 2026-11-15](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-map). I did not check this against Rockstar's own store.
- **Size (RUMOR/LEAK, conflicting):**
  - Several guides say Leonida is about twice the size of GTA V's map and about three times Red Dead Redemption 2's. They attribute this to Rockstar staff (Aaron Garbut and Rob Nelson). — [recharge.com](https://www.recharge.com/blog/en-gb/gta-6-map-leonida-vice-city-size-guide); [mmoexp](https://www.mmoexp.com/News/gta-6-map-2026-guide-leonida-regions-size-and-dangers-to-know.html)
  - One outlier puts it at about 1.7 times GTA V's map. — [buffbuff](https://www.buffbuff.com/blog/gta-6-map-guide)
  - Community estimates in km² range from about 83 to about 150 km². — the same search set; none of them is authoritative.
  - GTA Boss lists the official size as "TBC". — [GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-map)
- **Counties (RUMOR/LEAK):** Trailer footage names Kelly County, Mariana County, Leonard County (said to lie between Vice City and Lummox County) and Lummox County (the northern end). How the counties relate to the six regions is unconfirmed. — [GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-map). SVG places Kelly County north, as a backwoods area. — [SVG, 2026-11-18](https://www.svg.com/2321164/gta-6-map-limitation/)
- **Neighborhoods and landmarks by region**, as listed by GTA Boss (pre-launch, from trailers; LIKELY or RUMOR):
  - *Vice City:* Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka flea market, VC Port, Southside Vice City, Shore Court (a garage).
  - *Ambrosia:* the Allied Crystal sugar refinery.
  - *Not placed in a region:* Watson Bay (Paradise Garage), Gambit Bay, Washington Beach, Shore Drive, Lake Leonida (One-Eyed Willie's), Stockyard (Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo), Leonida Penitentiary, The Rusty Anchor, Delights Cabaret, Key Lento, Vercetti Estate, Ocean View Hotel and Hamlet.
  - Source: [GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-map)
- **Place names in post-launch collectible guides (LIKELY):** Key Lento (Leonida Keys), Port Gellhorn (including the Gellhorn Funfair Ferris wheel), Capri, Hamlet (with an Ammu-Nation and a billboard), Watson Bay, Belville, Starfish Island (mansions), Kalaga Reservoir and Selkie Hole (underwater tunnels). — [The Big Lead, 2026-11-18](https://www.thebiglead.com/gta-6-collectibles-guide-cards-styles-meteorite-chunks-landmark-scans/); [GTABoom](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-collectibles). games.gg warns that Capri should not be counted as a seventh region. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-map-unlock-how-to-reveal-full-map/)
- **Opening and unlock order (LIKELY):**
  - The map is technically open from the start, but the first ~30% of the story takes place in the Leonida Keys.
  - Lucia cannot leave the Keys until about 35% story completion.
  - Many side activities outside the Keys need items you get later, such as a mask for robberies.
  - A bridge north out of the Keys reaches Vice City in about 5–10 minutes of driving.
  - Source: [SVG, 2026-11-18](https://www.svg.com/2321164/gta-6-map-limitation/)
- **Fog of war (LIKELY):** Visiting an area clears the fog over it. A fogged area is unexplored, not locked. games.gg says there is no confirmed region-by-region unlock sequence. — [games.gg, 2026-11-18](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-map-unlock-how-to-reveal-full-map/)
- **Progress Database (LIKELY):** The in-game map has a Progress Database under Map > Progress that tracks every category. Some subcategories stay hidden until you find the first item in them. — [PowerPyx, 2026-11-18](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-collectible-guide/)

- **Size, from Rockstar via Edge magazine (LIKELY, close to official):**
  - Edge visited Rockstar North. It reports Leonida as roughly twice the size of GTA V's map.
  - It also reports the landmass as nearly as large as the GTA IV, GTA V and RDR2 maps combined, and says there are more interiors than in earlier Rockstar games.

  - Source: [TweakTown summarising Edge, 2026-10-29](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114382/gta-6s-map-is-roughly-twice-gta-5s-and-almost-as-big-as-gta-4-gta-5-and-rdr2-combined/index.html)

  - The figure of 83 km² comes from the 2022 leak or fan measurement (RUMOR/LEAK). — [Dexerto wiki](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/leonida-map-size/)
- **Counties are in the game (LIKELY, two independent post-launch sources):**

- **In-game sub-area names**, taken from the JFL stunt jump chapter list (video, 2026-11-20; LIKELY, single source that derives from MapGenie). Grouping into regions is my own inference from the chapter order and from other sources:
  - *Vice City area:* Balsero, Bayside, Bluegrass, Crosstown, Dalton Island, Decor District, Downtown, Hamlet, La Perle, Little Cuba, Ocean Beach, Penmarch Isle, Port VC, Salton, Southside, Tequesta, Tequesta Retreat, VCIA (Vice City International Airport) and VCIA Terminals, Washington Beach, plus South/West Vice-Dale County.

  - These can be tagged **CONFIRMED** as place names.

### Inferences
- The Keys, then Vice City, then the north is the natural order for the app to present regions. The app should not mark regions as locked; a "fog/unexplored" toggle per region fits better.
- Use the six official regions as the top-level taxonomy. Treat counties and neighborhoods as optional sub-tags until they are confirmed in-game.

### Gaps
- No official km² figure or region boundary polygons.
- No in-game coordinates.
- I could not find a confirmed list of neighborhoods per region from post-launch sources. The pre-launch lists come from trailers.
- I could not verify whether fast travel exists. No source I found describes it, so it is not yet known.

## 5. Interactive maps and reuse terms

### Takeaway
Several interactive maps exist. Only the in-game-tracker-based guides (PowerPyx) and post-launch fan trackers have real data; the pre-launch fan maps hold estimated positions. MapGenie (owned by IGN/Ziff Davis) explicitly forbids scraping, data sets and RAG use. The app should link to it and never ingest its data.

### Cited Findings
- **MapGenie:**
  - It has a GTA 6 map with filters for collectibles, weapons, vehicles and activities. — [Dexerto FR](https://www.dexerto.fr/gta/gta-6-carte-interactive-1664364/)
  - Its robots.txt carries the Ziff Davis notice. It bans automated scraping without written permission. It specifically bans use for AI/ML (training, embedding, RAG), for creating data sets, and for any commercial purpose. It also blocks many AI crawlers.
  - Its footer says it is an IGN Entertainment brand and that no content may be reproduced without permission.
  - Source: mapgenie.io/robots.txt and /terms, fetched 2026-11-22 (CONFIRMED)
  - IGN's own map: ign.com returned 403 to this researcher, so the content and terms are unverified. IGN is also Ziff Davis, so the same restrictions very likely apply.
- **PowerPyx:**
  - robots.txt is "User-agent: * Allow: /" (fetched 2026-11-22).
  - Its data is organized in a tree that mirrors the in-game Map > Progress database, with sub-guides for each category.
  - Source: [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-collectible-guide/)
- **GTABase:**
  - An interactive map with feature filters through the `?feature=` parameter, for example `hidden-packages`, `collectible-card` and `street-food`. It has progress tracking.
  - robots.txt allows the map pages but disallows /user/ and search. The page has no visible license.
  - Source: [GTABase map](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/map/)
- **GTA Boss (gtaboss.gg):**
  - The fan map has 6 category groups: Shops and Services, Vehicles, Properties, Activities, Travel and Emergency Services. Each marker has its own page.
  - The map says its marker positions are estimates.
  - robots.txt allows detail pages but blocks faceted query URLs for AI crawlers.
  - Source: [GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-map) (RUMOR/LEAK for marker positions)
- **Fan reconstruction maps from before launch:**
  - The "State of Leonida" / GTA6 mapping project had about 1,600 markers, built from trailers, screenshots and leaks. Its makers say it is only a reconstruction. — [GamingBible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-interactive-map-project-896423-20250521); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/full-gta-6-map-put-together-interactive-site/); [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Community-GTA-6-map-uses-Netflix-trailer-and-leaks-to-accurately-chart-Leonida.1390940.0.html) (RUMOR/LEAK)

  - The JFL video description credits it as the location source for its 130 stunt jumps, which shows MapGenie's map has post-launch stunt-jump data.
  - I did not fetch it; its robots.txt bans automated retrieval, and I did not use a Wayback snapshot for the same reason.
  - Category names and counts are therefore unverified here.
  - Source: [YouTube uq5T0aZgjFA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uq5T0aZgjFA)

  - Game8 has an interactive map of Leonida and Vice City (HTTP 402 to this researcher). — [Game8](https://game8.co/games/GTA-6/archives/638636)

It is the main pre-release fan mapping effort. — [GTAForums p.334](https://gtaforums.com/topic/985670-mapping-vice-city-map-discussion-thread-no-leak-footage-allowed/page/334/) (forum; 403 to this researcher, known from search snippet); [PC Gamer on the mapping effort](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/speculatively-plotting-gta-6s-map-is-a-painstaking-exhausting-and-heroic-effort-we-had-10-people-search-every-street-in-streetview-this-took-weeks-and-failed); [Rockstar Intel](https://rockstarintel.com/exclusive-mapping-gta-6-how-fans-uncover-rockstars-fictional-florida-one-screenshot-at-a-time/)

### Inferences
- Safest approach for the app: build pins from your own play, or from player contributions checked against the in-game Progress counts.

### Gaps
