# Grand Theft Auto VI Weapons (as of November 22, 2026)

Researched 2026-11-22. GTA VI launched November 19, 2026, single-player only, to a Metacritic score of 99 ([Al Jazeera, 2026-11-19](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/grand-theft-auto-vi-launches-to-rave-reviews-after-13-year-wait); [Push Square, 2025-11, delay to Nov 19 2026](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/11/gta-6-delayed-again-pushed-back-to-november-2026)). It has been out for three days, so post-launch weapon data is thin and comes mostly from one wiki (Dexerto). Many "guides" ranking high in search were written before launch and are still up; each is flagged below.

Confidence tags: **CONFIRMED** = official, or several reputable guides agree after launch. **LIKELY** = one reputable post-launch source. **RUMOR/LEAK** = trailer or leak analysis, pre-launch speculation, or a low-reputation or conflicting fan guide.

## 1. Full launch weapon list and how stat bars are shown

### Takeaway
The launch arsenal is large: Dexerto ranks 136 weapons and GTABase lists 206 including more than 120 "Unique Weapon Variants." Most entries are named variants of a smaller set of base guns (K17, ES9, 556, KZ-59, Vatra and others). Dexerto shows four numeric stats per gun: Damage, Accuracy, Handling and Range. Values have one decimal place, look like a 0–10 scale, and the scale is never stated. Dexerto lists no fire-rate stat. No second reputable source has published per-gun stat numbers yet, so every number below is **LIKELY** (one source).

### Cited Findings

**Weapon counts (sources disagree)**
- Dexerto's tier pages rank weapons "of 136" overall, 25 pistols and 21 assault rifles. LIKELY. Source: [Dexerto Klose K17 page, published 2026-11-19](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/weapons/pistols/klose-k17/); [Dexerto Duke 556 page, 2026-11-19](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/weapons/assault-rifles/duke-556/)

- There seems to be no fire-rate bar. Fire style (semi, burst, full auto) may be what the game shows instead.
- Most "Unique Variants" are re-skinned or tuned versions of base guns, named for gangs, characters or places (for example "Marabunta Grande KZ-59" and "Gil's Kalinga .50"). The database should probably carry a `base_weapon` field.

## 2. Weapon wheel, carry limits, ammo capacity, customization, gun shops and prices

### Takeaway
GTA VI replaces unlimited carrying with a loadout, and overflow weapons are stored in your vehicle trunk or hideout. Sources disagree on the exact slots. Gun shops are Ammu-Nation, in four stock tiers. Sniper rifles and launchers, and their ammo, are sold only at "expanded" stores. Attachments are fitted at Ammu-Nation, and some can be removed in the field. Unique Variants reportedly cannot be modified. I found no ammo capacity caps and no attachment prices.

### Cited Findings

**Carry limits and weapon wheel**
- Loadout is "two handguns (concealed) and two long guns (visible)." LIKELY. Source: [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/weapons/gta-6/)
- Conflicting report: two long weapons, one pistol and one melee weapon. Extra guns go in the vehicle trunk or hideout, and "lose the car and you lose the weapons inside it." RUMOR/LEAK: written before launch, hedged as "reported." Source: [AllThingsHow, updated 2026-09-02](https://allthings.how/gta-6-weapon-system-how-the-new-loadout-limit-works/)
- An earlier Dexerto news report said GTA 6 would use a loadout system instead of unlimited carrying. RUMOR/LEAK: pre-launch. Source: [Dexerto news](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-will-use-new-loadout-system-instead-of-carrying-unlimited-weapons-3379414/)
- The wheel reportedly has three groups (Weapons, Equipment, Gear) cycled with R1/RB, and attachments can be fitted from the wheel. RUMOR/LEAK. Source: [AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/gta-6-weapon-system-how-the-new-loadout-limit-works/)
- One guide said Rockstar confirmed a limit exists but has published no official number. RUMOR/LEAK: secondhand, no Rockstar link. Source: [Timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/gta-6-weapons-weapon-wheel-carry-limit)

**Ammo**
- Ammo types reported: Pistol, Shotgun, Heavy, Spear, Rockets. LIKELY (GTABase and AllThingsHow agree, but AllThingsHow is pre-launch). Source: [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/weapons/gta-6/); [AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/gta-6-weapon-system-how-the-new-loadout-limit-works/)
- Calibers do vary by gun (9mm, 5.56×45mm and others per Dexerto), which suggests ammo may be tracked by caliber rather than in the five pools above. This conflicts with the five-type list and is unresolved. Source: [Dexerto Klose K17](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/weapons/pistols/klose-k17/)

- Body armor and a parachute are sold at Ammu-Nation; in the first Key Lento store, armor is on the right wall and the parachute on the back-left wall. LIKELY. Source: same.
- Some weapons need a bank account before they can be bought (for example the Duke 556). LIKELY. Source: [Dexerto Duke 556](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/weapons/assault-rifles/duke-556/)

## 3. Best weapons per class and community tier lists

### Takeaway
The only stat-based post-launch tier system found is Dexerto's S–D tiers, built from its numeric stats. Other "tier lists" are mostly pre-launch and based on trailers. I found no reputable post-launch community consensus (Reddit, IGN, GameRant) in three days.

### Cited Findings
- Dexerto publishes separate tier lists for Best Weapons, Highest Damage, Most Accurate, Best Handling and Longest Range, overall and per class, across 136 weapons. In it, the Klose K17 is B overall and S for handling (10th of 136), and the Duke 556 is A overall (41st of 136) and B among assault rifles (8th of 21). LIKELY. Source: [Dexerto K17](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/weapons/pistols/klose-k17/); [Dexerto Duke 556](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/weapons/assault-rifles/duke-556/)
- Guides recommend the Klose K17 as the best pistol, the Hawk & Little Morgan revolver as a standout, the Duke 556 or a "Carbine Rifle" as the assault rifle pick, and the grenade launcher and Molotov for heavy and throwable. RUMOR/LEAK: several guides admit they rank by how prominent a gun is in reveals rather than by stats. Dexerto's post-launch data contradicts the K17 pick (C-tier among pistols). Sources: [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/gta/best-weapons-in-gta-6/); [games.gg tier list](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/best-weapons-grand-theft-auto-6-tier-list/); [Roonby](https://roonby.com/2026/11/19/gta-6-best-weapons-guide-best-guns-for-every-weapon-class/amp/)
- One site grades only the 7 guns Rockstar named, puts the Hawk & Little Morgan revolver at the top grade, and says its grades are not gameplay claims. RUMOR/LEAK. Source: search snippet; the page was likely [games.gg tier list](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/best-weapons-grand-theft-auto-6-tier-list/) or [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/gta/best-weapons-in-gta-6/), and I did not fetch either.

### Inferences
- Until a second stat source appears, the app could pull Dexerto's per-category tier and rank as a `dexerto_tier` field and sort on its four numeric stats. Label it as single-source.

### Gaps
- Dexerto's S-tier names per class: the tier pages were not fetched individually.
- No r/GTA6 tier list or stat compilation found.
- No IGN, GameSpot or GameRant post-launch best-weapons article found.
