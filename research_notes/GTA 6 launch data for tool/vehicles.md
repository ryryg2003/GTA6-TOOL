# GTA VI Vehicles (cars, bikes, boats, aircraft) — as of Nov 22, 2026

Research date: 2026-11-22. Game reportedly launched 2026-11-19 (PS5 / Xbox Series X|S). Search results did not include an official Rockstar launch confirmation, but many dated post-launch guides (Nov 19–22) describe live gameplay, so launch is treated as having happened.

Confidence tags: **CONFIRMED** = official, or in-game data reported the same way by 2+ independent reputable post-launch guides. **LIKELY** = one reputable post-launch source. **RUMOR/LEAK** = trailer analysis, leaks, fan/SEO sites, unverified.

Source-quality warning: launch-week coverage is dominated by fast-built fan and SEO databases (meowdb, gameta, gtabase, gtabites, gta6vehicles.org, gta-zone, etc.). Many mix in GTA V names, contradict each other, or call themselves WIP. The vehicle *names* that come straight from in-game menus (meowdb, Dexerto, Push Square, GameRant, GTA Boss) are more reliable than the "real-life equivalent" lists.

## 1. Vehicle list at launch, and how in-game stats are represented

### Takeaway
No official or complete vehicle list has been published. Fan databases give totals from about 94 to 641, and none of them agree. The in-game tuning/stat menu uses **0–10 bars** for Speed, Acceleration, Asphalt Handling, Off-Road Handling and Strength, plus a derived **Road (Asphalt) Rating** and **Off-Road Rating** in the hundreds. GTA VI shows **no mph/km/h top speeds and no Braking stat**, and as of Nov 22 no community-measured top speeds exist.

### Cited Findings

**Stat representation**
- In the mod shop the game shows these stats before you buy: Speed, Acceleration, Asphalt Handling, Off-Road Handling, Strength, and a Rating. **LIKELY** (2026-11-20) — [GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-car-customization)
- meowdb scores Speed, Acceleration, Asphalt, Off-road and Strength out of 10. It says the figures come from the tuning menu (rows tagged "Reported") and gives a road-rating formula: 20.9×Speed + 40.7×Accel + 37.9×Asphalt + 7.4. It has no formula for the off-road rating. Page dated 2026-11-22 and marked WIP. **LIKELY** — [meowdb](https://meowdb.com/gta6/best-cars)
- Cross-check: Dexerto's HVY Wastelander page gives Speed 3.1, Accel 3.8, Asphalt 2.6, Off-Road 7, Strength 3.5, Asphalt Rating 328 and Off-Road Rating 535. These match meowdb exactly, so the stat format and the values are treated as **CONFIRMED** (two independent sources, 2026-11-20/22) — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/vehicles/cars/off-road-vehicles/hvy-wastelander/); [meowdb](https://meowdb.com/gta6/best-cars)
- Holding LEFT on the d-pad while driving shows vehicle stats on the HUD. **LIKELY** (2026-11-19) — [GTABase fuel article](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-fuel-system-explained-how-vehicle-refuelling-works)
- Most vehicles have Strength 3.0. Game8 lists off-road scores such as Black Moth Kamacho 8.3 and Smuggler Caracara 7.6, and gameta lists Love Buggy Outlaw 8.2 and Stolen Valor 300-H Strength 4.0. **RUMOR/LEAK** (search snippets, not fetched) — [Game8](https://game8.co/games/GTA-6/archives/639240), [gameta](https://gameta.gg/gta6/vehicles/standout/)
- GTA Bites uses its own 0–100 handling score (for example Nagasaki Outlaw 82/100) that doesn't map onto the in-game 0–10 bars. Treat it as non-canonical. **RUMOR/LEAK** — [GTA Bites](https://www.gtabites.com/vehicles/off-road)

**"Standout" (named-variant) vehicles**
- Databases separate base models from "Standout" vehicles. Standouts are named unique variants with their own liveries or builds, such as "Smuggler Caracara", "Black Moth Kamacho", "Royal Ruby Emerus" and "Bloodbath Phoenix". GTABase's Nov 22 changelog added 30 of them, including Valiant Tempesta, Tsunami Zorrusso, Vice Vinyl Coquette, Yago's Elegy, Turbo Ruiner, Will O' The Wisp Airboat, Westbound Azure (boat) and JoBuilt 1776 Phantom. **LIKELY** — [GTABase vehicles](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/vehicles/); [gameta standout list](https://gameta.gg/gta6/vehicles/standout/)
- GTABase gives two different totals on the same page ("all 641 GTA 6 vehicles" in the intro, "around 300 vehicles" in the FAQ). Other databases report 258, or 277 base plus 280 Standouts. **Counts are unreliable.** — [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/vehicles/); [search summary of tracker.gg / timesaver.gg / gta6gang](https://tracker.gg/gta6/vehicles)

### Inferences
- The in-game menu leads with Speed and Acceleration bars rather than mph, and it has a separate "Off-Road Handling" bar, which together suggest Rockstar designed around Leonida's mixed terrain (swamps, Mariana/Kelly counties). Your app schema should store `speed_bar`, `accel_bar`, `asphalt_handling`, `offroad_handling`, `strength`, `road_rating`, `offroad_rating` and a `modded` flag, plus optional nullable `top_speed_mph` and `braking`.
- The manufacturer names differ between The Tab and in-game menus (Piccia vs Grotti Furia, Granforma vs Pegassi Zorrusso, Buckingham vs Enus Jubilee, Emperor vs Karin Sultan, Mer-Can vs Nagasaki Outlaw). This strongly suggests The Tab's list is partly carried over from GTA V or built from trailers. Prefer in-game menu names.

### Gaps
- No official Rockstar vehicle list or count.
- No seat counts, braking stats, or per-vehicle register costs were found in any fetched source.
- No stat data was found for boats or aircraft. meowdb covers land vehicles only.
- Pre-release trailer IDs were not separately compiled. Sportskeeda's trailer-based list itself admits it is not 100% accurate ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/gta-6-vehicles-list-cars-returning-previous-games)).

## 2. Fastest vehicles per class and community-tested top speeds

### Takeaway
As of Nov 22 nobody has measured top speeds (no Broughy1322-style mph tests found). The mph figures in circulation are pre-launch estimates. By in-game Speed bar, the fastest land vehicles reported are the modded Pegassi '96 Infernus (8.6) and the stock Pegassi Vitello (8.2).

### Cited Findings
- Fastest by Speed bar, per class (all from [meowdb](https://meowdb.com/gta6/best-cars), 2026-11-22, **LIKELY**):
  - **Super/Car:** Pegassi '96 Infernus 8.6 (modded). The stock leader is Pegassi Vitello at 8.2, which also has the highest road rating in the list (832).
  - **Muscle:** Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire 7.8.
  - **Motorcycle:** Nagasaki SFB 1000 7.1, with a road rating of 796, second highest overall.
  - **EV:** Pfister Neon 7.1.
  - **SUV:** Pegassi Toros 6.0.
  - **Truck:** Vapid Ganado 4.7.
  - **Off-road (by off-road rating):** Mer-Can Outlaw 727 and BF '77 Bandito 726 (modded). Highest off-road handling bar: Mer-Can Outlaw 8.4.
- ProGameGuides ranks the Grotti Itali RSX first at about 135 mph and the Pegassi Tempesta at about 128 mph. It sources these to "GTA 6 Bible" estimates, says it will replace them with measured figures later, and is dated 2026-11-19. **RUMOR/LEAK**. The Itali RSX also doesn't appear in meowdb's in-game list. — [ProGameGuides (search snippet; page returned 403)](https://progameguides.com/gta/fastest-cars-in-gta-6-vehicle-top-speed/)
- One guide gives an estimated 155 mph for a "Principe Alvino V1" motorcycle. This is unmeasured. **RUMOR/LEAK** — [search summary](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-fastest-cars-ranked-list-by-top-speed)
- Some "fastest" pages show GTA V stock speeds (for example Itali GTO 117 mph), and RankedBoost warns that these don't carry over. **Do not use.** — [RankedBoost](https://rankedboost.com/gta/fastest-cars/)

### Inferences
- Until a GPS or speedometer-tested community list appears, the app should rank by the Speed bar or road rating and label the result "in-game rating, not mph".

### Gaps
- No community-tested mph/km/h data. No fastest boat, plane or helicopter data.

## 4. Ownership, garages, insurance/impound, mod shops, fuel and damage

### Takeaway
There are **no conventional dealerships**. You steal a car, then pay to **register** it with Moses (Port Gellhorn) or later at a Pay 'n' Spray, and store it in a garage that holds one car per garage. Garages are bought in the **Vice City Real Estate app**. Fuel/EV charge and engine damage are real systems. Tuning is split across specialty mod shops, not one Los Santos Customs chain.

### Cited Findings

**Ownership and registration**
- To own a car, steal one without a tracker and register it at Moses's garage in Port Gellhorn (from Chapter 2) or at a Pay 'n' Spray (from Chapter 3). Multiple guides agree. **CONFIRMED** — [GameRant 2026-11-21](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-store-cars-garage-little-boxes/); [search summary of metaforge/theclick/thegta6map](https://metaforge.app/gta-6/how-to-get-a-car-gta-6)
- Some expensive cars have trackers Moses can't remove, and more registration services unlock as the story progresses. Reports say the first registration is free and high-end cars cost over $20,000 to register. **LIKELY/RUMOR** (search snippets) — [Game8](https://game8.co/games/GTA-6/archives/639287); [thegta6map](https://thegta6map.com/articles/gta-6-car-dealerships-impound)
- The only dealer-style purchase: **Status Auto Imports**, where Jason and Lucia buy a high-end car during the Chapter 4 mission "Company Cars". **LIKELY** — [search summary (Game8 et al.)](https://game8.co/games/GTA-6/archives/639287)
- Wyman's Classic Cars sells the Mamba GT with the Ultimate Edition. **LIKELY** — [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-randys-wish-list-vehicle-locations)
- If you run out of fuel, Joey (from Contacts) can deliver your personal vehicle. **LIKELY** — [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-fuel-system-explained-how-vehicle-refuelling-works)

**Garages** (GameRant, 2026-11-21, **LIKELY**; prices conflict with other guides). There are 14 garages: 8 to buy, 4 from the story, 2 from editions or preorder, plus a reported 15th for GTA+. Each adds **one** slot. Buying all 8 costs $200,000, or $160,000 with the 20% BuckMe Gold discount (requires a $100k deposit). — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-store-cars-garage-little-boxes/)
