## Story missions (order, chapters, count, missables, ratings) — SPOILERS

### Takeaway
The story is a Prologue plus Chapters 1–5. PowerPyx lists 149 mission entries, about 30 of them optional. There are no gold medals or mission ratings and no mission replay. A few optional, time-sensitive missions are missable. The list below comes from one comprehensive source, cross-checked against Fandom for Chapter 1. The order can vary slightly.

## Trophies / achievements

### Takeaway
There are 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. On Xbox it's 55 achievements worth 1,000G, including a "Mythic" achievement. All are single-player and there are no online or DLC trophies. Difficulty is about 4/10 and the platinum takes about 200 hours in a single playthrough. One trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, may be missable.

## Activities, side hustles, properties, customization

### Takeaway
Activities include races, drone races, stunts, shooting ranges, pool, mini golf, basketball, arcade games, fishing, hunting (Bret's decks), diving (Kenton), meditation, WAiNK scanning and many robbery types. There are 8 garages to buy, about $160k in total, through a Vice City Real Estate app. Safehouses come from the story. I found no confirmed income-generating business ownership. Clothing, barbers and tattoos are in the base game, and some premium shops are Ultimate Edition only.

## Beginner tips

### Cited Findings (all LIKELY; [PowerPyx tips](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-tips-tricks/) unless noted)
- Link BuckMe so you don't need to carry cash. Taxi fast travel costs $100 or more.
- Good early money comes from Gang Operations and, from mid-Ch3, store robberies. Stick-ups pay poorly. Fence options improve as the story goes on.
- Don't over-deposit into family savings because it can't be withdrawn.
- Saving manually and reloading repairs your vehicle for free.
- Losing police: sports bikes help, train tracks help against helicopters at 4+ stars, and a Pay 'n' Spray respray after leaving the search area clears heat. Hats, bandanas and glasses lower recognition, and holding Circle while driving makes you duck.
- Turn on every progress and collectible notification. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go. Avoid the permanent "Irredeemable" criminal profile — [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/achievements)
- Keep cheats off on a trophy run — [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/achievements)

## Data sources for the daily researcher (reliability, freshness, access)

### Takeaway
The best automation targets are the Fandom MediaWiki API (structured, with timestamps; check Fandom's terms), the PowerPyx RSS feed (updated several times a day, `robots.txt` allows all), and GTABase (structured databases, has `llms.txt`, and its `robots.txt` doesn't block GTA VI pages). IGN, MapGenie (both Ziff Davis) and GameRant (Valnet) explicitly forbid automated scraping and RAG in `robots.txt`. Link to them only. Rockstar Newswire has no RSS (404) and blocks ClaudeBot and GPTBot. Poll it with a regular HTML fetch at low frequency, or rely on news coverage of it.

### Cited Findings: robots.txt and APIs (checked directly 2026-11-22)
- **PowerPyx**: `User-agent: * Allow: /`, with a sitemap at `/sitemap_index.xml`. The RSS feed at `https://www.powerpyx.com/feed/` returns `application/rss+xml`, and its newest items are timestamped 2026-11-22 20:28 UTC (several GTA VI posts per day). Reliability is high: dedicated trophy, walkthrough and 100% guides. CONFIRMED — [robots](https://www.powerpyx.com/robots.txt); [feed](https://www.powerpyx.com/feed/)

Reliability is medium-high because it's community-edited. CONFIRMED (tested) — [API](https://gta.fandom.com/api.php?action=query&list=recentchanges&rclimit=3&format=json). Content is CC BY-SA, so attribute it. I didn't re-verify the licence this session; that's inferred.

### Cited Findings: forums, community and creative routes (tested 2026-11-22 from this sandbox)

Use them only to corroborate a guide or wiki. CONFIRMED (tested) — [ou4vff0wxhM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ou4vff0wxhM); [9DFDdPQjraU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9DFDdPQjraU). ### Recommended field schemas (based on fields the sources commonly expose)
- **common**: `id, name, slug, summary_own_words (≤280 chars), sources[] {url, source_type, retrieved_at, publish_date}, independent_source_count, first_seen, last_verified, confidence (CONFIRMED|LIKELY|RUMOR), disputed (bool) + dispute_note, spoiler (bool), game_version`
- **video_evidence** (optional, attached to missions): `video_id, channel, upload_date, chapter_title_raw, start_time_s`. Store only the timestamp and our own label, never transcript text.
- **mission**: `chapter (0=Prologue..5), order_index, type (story|optional|heist_setup|stranger|character_quest), character_lock (jason|lucia|either|solo), heist_group (cartel|vcia|null), missable (bool), time_sensitive (bool), unlocks[], prerequisites[], linked_trophy_id, reward_cash (nullable)`. No medal fields, since there are no gold medals.
- **trophy**: `platform_names {psn, xbox}, grade (P|G|S|B), gamerscore, hidden (bool), category (story|activity|collectible|crime|misc|character_quest), requirement_count, missable (bool|"possibly"), related_entity_ids[], rarity_pct (nullable)`
- **checklist_item (100%)**: `group (story_tasks|exploration_activities|discovery), required, total, unit, missable`
- **map pin / collectible**: `category (meditation|card|flamingo|magazine|underwater|basketball|drone|stunt|garage|shop|gang_op|bank|vault|...), region, coords {x,y} or map_provider_ref, character_lock, requires_chapter, repeatable/respawns`
- **property**: `kind (garage|safehouse|business), acquisition (purchase|story|edition|gta_plus|preorder), price, capacity, region, edition_exclusive`
- **vehicle**: `class, manufacturer, base_model (bool), price, top_speed, scan_required_for_100 (bool), where_found, mod_shop_type`
- **weapon**: `class, price, ammunation (bool), standard_issue (bool), attachments[], stats {damage, fire_rate, range, accuracy}`
- **event / patch**: `title, date, platform[], version (e.g. 01.002.001), size_mb per platform, official_notes_url (nullable), source_type (rockstar|press|datamine)`
- **shop / customization**: `type (clothing|barber|tattoo|mod_shop), brand, region, edition_exclusive, shared_inventory_brand`
