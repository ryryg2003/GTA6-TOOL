# GTA VI: Release Status, Patches, Characters and Systems (as of Nov 22, 2026)

Confidence tags: **CONFIRMED** = Rockstar/platform-holder official, or several reputable outlets agree; **LIKELY** = one reputable source or several post-launch guides that agree; **RUMOR/LEAK** = forums, leaks, unverified claims, pre-release speculation. Source dates are given where known. Fan/aggregator guide sites are marked [community/aggregator].

Research caveats: GameSpot (403) and the Rockstar Support pre-order page (503) blocked fetches, and RockstarINTEL rate-limited them (429). Facts from those pages come from search snippets only. Wikipedia figures are as of about Nov 21, 2026 and cite underlying outlets. Treat Wikipedia as a secondary source.

## 1. Launch status: date, platforms, editions, price, size, PC

### Takeaway
GTA VI launched on schedule on **Nov 19, 2026**, for **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only**. Unlock was at local midnight. The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the reported Ultimate Edition is $99.99. The install is about 210 GB. There is no PC version, and Rockstar has not announced a PC date.

Alongside it came *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album* (Atlantic Records, 34 original tracks). — [Finviz/Business Wire, Nov 19 2026](https://finviz.com/news/425095/rockstar-games-announces-grand-theft-auto-vi-now-available)

## 3. Protagonists, voice actors, switching

### Takeaway
There are two playable leads, **Jason Duval** (voiced by Dylan Rourke) and **Lucia Caminos** (voiced by Manni L. Perez). You can switch between them in free roam after a story unlock, though guides disagree on the exact timing and button. No third playable protagonist is reported.

### Cited Findings
**Official/pre-release character background (Rockstar character pages, 2025)**
- CONFIRMED (pre-release): Lucia's father taught her to fight. She served time at the Leonida Penitentiary and wants a better life. Jason grew up around grifters, served in the army, and works for local drug runners in the Leonida Keys. The pair meet and fall into crime together in a Bonnie-and-Clyde style story. — [Rockstar GTA VI People page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/people); [Wikipedia (Bloomberg/Schreier reporting)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)
- CONFIRMED: The official launch materials say Jason and Lucia are caught up in a criminal conspiracy across Leonida and must rely on each other. — [Finviz/Business Wire](https://finviz.com/news/425095/rockstar-games-announces-grand-theft-auto-vi-now-available)
- LIKELY: Voice actors are **Dylan Rourke** (Jason) and **Manni L. Perez** (Lucia). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)
- LIKELY: Lucia is widely described as the series' first female lead protagonist. Wikipedia notes that earlier games had optional female characters. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)

**Switching (post-launch guides)**
- LIKELY: In free roam you can play the two as a couple or split them up, and their relationship responds to what you do. — [Wikipedia, citing PC Gamer Aug 26 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)

## 4. Stats / skills system

### Takeaway
GTA VI drops GTA V's seven-skill system. Each protagonist has **three stats: Strength (max health), Cardio (max stamina) and Focus (slow-motion aiming time)**, each capped at **Level 4**. You train them through workouts with timed "Workout Windows". Missed windows make Strength and Cardio decay, while Focus levels are permanent.

### Cited Findings
- LIKELY: Stats and their effects:
  - **Strength** (pink heart icon) raises maximum health and visibly builds muscle.
  - **Cardio** (green bolt icon) raises maximum stamina.
  - **Focus** (purple eye icon) sets how long you can slow time while aiming.
  - All three max out at **Level 4**, and each character has their own.
  - You can view them in the in-game **Fitness app** and in the pause menu.
  - Sources: [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/workout-guide/); [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-increase-stats-in-gta-6/); [MetaForge [community]](https://metaforge.app/gta-6/how-to-increase-stats-in-gta-6-strength-cardio-and-focus)
- LIKELY: How to train each stat. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/workout-guide/)
  - **Strength:** weights on the terrace at Jason's house in Chapter 1, and paid gym memberships later.
  - **Cardio:** the punching bag at Jason's house, and treadmills or bikes at fitness studios. Running, cycling and swimming in free roam also count.
  - **Focus:** shooting-range challenges, plus golf, pool and fishing. Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range in Kelly County, northeast of Port Gellhorn, is cited as an early and fast option.
  - **Partner workouts** unlock later and let you train both characters in one visit.
- LIKELY: Decay and recovery. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/workout-guide/)
  - Strength and Cardio each have their own timed Workout Window. If the window closes before you finish a session, that session's gains are lost.
  - Repeatedly missing windows makes your levels drop.
  - Focus has no window and builds up across sessions.
  - After a workout comes a "Rest and Recover" cooldown. Protein shakes or bars shorten it, and so does sleeping at a safehouse. Eating before a session gives extra stamina for sets.
- LIKELY: Rockstar's Rob Nelson is quoted as saying that three exercises a day for three days in a row levels up health and strength. Food and drinks give temporary stamina, and **Adrenaline** temporarily boosts Health, Stamina and Focus. — [search summary of guides incl. ThePhraseMaker Nov 19 2026](https://thephrasemaker.com/2026/11/19/all-stats-how-to-increase-them-in-gta-6/)
- LIKELY: There are fitness challenges called **"Strength Regime"** and **"Cardio Regime"**. Both require both characters to hold Level 4 in that stat for five consecutive in-game days. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/workout-guide/)
- LIKELY: Body weight can go up or down, and staying awake for days shows on a character's face. — [Wikipedia, citing Eurogamer Aug 27 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)

### Inferences
- There is no evidence of separate Shooting, Driving, Flying, Stealth or Lung Capacity stats in GTA VI. Guides that list them are describing GTA Online or GTA V. — [Fandom: GTA Online skills, for contrast](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Skills_in_GTA_Online)

### Gaps
- No sourced per-character bonuses or GTA V-style special abilities. Focus works like a shared Dead-Eye/slow-motion mechanic, but no source assigns either character a unique ability.
- The exact XP needed per level was not found.

## 5. Health/armor, wanted/police, combat, heists, relationships, phone/social

### Takeaway
GTA VI pairs the familiar star-based wanted level with a persistent **Criminal Profile** reputation system. Police respond only to crimes someone witnesses. Loadouts replace the weapon wheel, safehouses work as bonding hubs, and the phone carries **Snapmatic** and a **Fitness app**.

### Cited Findings
- LIKELY: Wanted level versus Criminal Profile. — [Wikipedia, citing Polygon Aug 27 & GamesRadar+ Nov 18 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI); [G2A News](https://www.g2a.com/news/gta-6-news/gta-6-criminal-profiles-explained/)
  - Stars track the current crime. The Criminal Profile is a long-term record of how violently and recklessly you play, and it changes consequences and how NPCs react.
  - Losing your stars does not reset the profile.
  - Tiers named in guides run from **Professional** up to **Psycho** and **Irredeemable**. Earlier previews also used the names Aggressive and Violent, so the final tier names are disputed.
- LIKELY: Rob Nelson told Famitsu that someone has to witness a crime, and bystanders can call the police. A worse profile brings faster police response and more aggressive police at every star level. According to Nelson, pushing the profile far enough breaks it for the rest of that playthrough. — [search summary: GameLeap](https://www.gameleap.com/articles/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-system-explained); [CULTR](https://www.cultr.com/news/gta-6-wanted-level-explained-how-the-star-system-works-and-how-to-get-6-stars/)
- RUMOR/LIKELY (community guides): "Heat" icons under the stars show what police know about you. At six stars you can no longer lose heat or clear the wanted level. The number of stars is disputed between sources. — [meowdb [community]](https://meowdb.com/gta6/wanted-level); [grandtheft.gg [community]](https://grandtheft.gg/gta-6-wanted-level-system/)
- LIKELY: You carry two primary firearms and two sidearms. Other weapons stay in the vehicle's trunk. Rockstar reworked loadouts to replace the weapon wheel. — [Wikipedia, citing TechRadar Oct 20 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)
- LIKELY: Health comes from Strength, and food and Adrenaline give temporary buffs (see section 4). No sourced details on armor changes were found.
- LIKELY: Safehouses work like the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2, as places to build the bond between the characters. The relationship between Jason and Lucia responds to player actions. A menu tab shows each character's profile and the state of their relationship. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI); [search summary of community guides](https://gmtreks.com/gta6/page/01M7SKPRZPD6GZNEJAH8PWYFR4)
- LIKELY: **Snapmatic** returns as the in-game social media app, parodying TikTok and Instagram. The phone plays radio and on-demand podcasts on foot (the podcasts are CONFIRMED by PlayStation Blog). The phone also has a Fitness app. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI); [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/18/grand-theft-auto-vi-launches-tomorrow-november-19/); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/workout-guide/)
- LIKELY: Activities include skydiving, wrestling, basketball, exercising, kayaking, scuba diving, golf, pool and fishing. The weather simulates tropical storms and hurricanes (IGN, Sep 29 2026). NPCs have about 600k animations, compared with 55k in GTA V (GamesRadar+). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)

### Gaps
- No reliable sourced detail on cover-system changes, heist planning structure, or armor values.
