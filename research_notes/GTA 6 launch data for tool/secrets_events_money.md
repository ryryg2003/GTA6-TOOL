# GTA VI: Secret Events, Easter Eggs and Mysteries, Money Methods, Cheats and Exploits (as of 2026-11-22)

Context: GTA VI launched on November 19, 2026 (PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, single-player at launch). Every source below is 0 to 3 days post-launch, so the information is volatile. Confidence tags:
- CONFIRMED: multiple reputable post-launch sources, or video evidence.
- LIKELY: a single reputable source.
- RUMOR/THEORY: forums or unverified sources.

Source-quality warning: search results are flooded with low-quality or SEO fan sites (e.g. gtabites.com, gta6cheatcodes.net, vicecityhustle.com, mapmaster.io, plus many "GTA 6 wiki" clones). Some of them recycle GTA V cheat codes (PAINKILLER, TURTLE) or contain garbled text. They are excluded, or tagged RUMOR where used. Reputable outlets I could actually read: VICE, Dexerto, Insider Gaming, PowerPyx, TweakTown, GamingBible. GameSpot returned HTTP 403. The GamesRadar article was truncated and could not be read. I found no IGN coverage on these topics.

## Random / world events (strangers, encounters, crimes in progress)

### Takeaway
Random events are unmarked on the map, and interactive ones reportedly never respawn once completed. To find them, travel slowly and use Focus (L3+R3) to locate shouting or gunfire. No authoritative full list exists yet. The named Strangers & Freaks questlines are well documented by PowerPyx and include a few missable, time-sensitive strands.

### Cited Findings
- **Discovery mechanic (LIKELY):** Events are not shown on the map. Drive or walk slowly rather than fast-travel, listen for shouting or sirens, and click both sticks to enter Focus, which points toward the source — [GTA Codex](https://www.gtacodex.com/discover/gta-6-random-events), [WikiGTAVI](https://www.wikigtavi.com/guides/gta-6-random-events-stranger-encounters) (fan sites, accessed 2026-11-22). Insider Gaming independently describes Focus as L3+R3 and an event triggered by "gunshots and roaring when passing by" — [Insider Gaming, 2026-11-19](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-early-game-locations-make-money/).
- **Scale (RUMOR):** One fan guide credits developers with "close to 500 ambient events across 12,500 authored locations" and "over 1,000 encounters"; a fan map lists 181 random-event locations. The developer quotes are unverified secondhand — [timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/gta-6-random-events), [GTABase map](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/map/?feature=random-event) (accessed 2026-11-22).
- **One-and-done (RUMOR):** Interactive encounters reportedly never respawn once finished, and some reportedly grow into quest lines — [timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/gta-6-random-events) (accessed 2026-11-22).

  - Dog on a jet ski (CONFIRMED, two sources): a goggled, life-jacketed dog rescues you if you are stranded at sea — [GamingBible, 2026-11-20](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-778903-20261120); also cited in the [random events search aggregate / GTA Codex](https://www.gtacodex.com/discover/gta-6-random-events).

## Hidden locations, easter eggs, past-GTA references, community mysteries

### Takeaway
Several easter eggs are already documented: a Mount Chiliad conversation, Bigfoot (Skunk Ape) near Cottonmouth Lake, a bird's nest that triggers a cutscene, and a Dexter apartment nod. The community's leading "Chiliad 2.0" candidate is a horse monument with star-chart-like symbols. Nothing has been solved.

### Cited Findings
- **Mount Chiliad conversation (CONFIRMED, two outlets):** Early in the story, when Jason meets Donnie/Donny and Brian at a bar, two patrons outside discuss Mount Chiliad and a hidden bunker with jetpacks and eggs. This references GTA V's Chiliad mural (UFO, jetpack, egg). Players also link the bunker to GTA Online's Doomsday Heist — [GamingBible Chiliad article, 2026-11-20](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-gta-5-mount-chiliad-mystery-easter-egg-802897-20261120); [GamingBible 9 easter eggs, 2026-11-20](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-778903-20261120). It is unknown whether the dialogue is guaranteed for every player.
- **Horse monument "Chiliad successor" (THEORY):** Near Manifest Canyon, reported by GamingBible as the "Monument of Leonidan Supremacy" north of Port Gellhorn. Four horse statues carry unusual, star-chart-like symbols, and one has a traffic cone on its head (possibly a nod to Glasgow's Duke of Wellington statue). Approaching it reportedly makes the controller vibrate and triggers beeping. Unsolved — [GamingBible, 2026-11-20](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-778903-20261120).
- **Conspiracy truck with an eye symbol (RUMOR):** A truck with conspiracy messages and an eye symbol, echoing the Chiliad mystery — search aggregate of fan guides incl. [outputlag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-easter-eggs/) (accessed 2026-11-22).
- **"Ghost of Vice City" treasure hunt (RUMOR, likely pre-release speculation):** Page dated ~160 days pre-launch; treat as unreliable — [games.gg](https://games.gg/grand-theft-auto-vi/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-easter-eggs-and-secrets-known-clues/).
- **Bigfoot / Skunk Ape (CONFIRMED as content):** A player chased Bigfoot in the woods near Cottonmouth Lake to a foul-smelling effigy. Bigfoot has been teased since San Andreas — [GamingBible, 2026-11-20](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-778903-20261120). Corroborated by Kirk's questline "Search for the Skunk Ape" / "Search for Tree Effigies" — [PowerPyx, 2026-11-18](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-strangers-freaks-side-missions/).
- **Bird's nest eggs (LIKELY):** At the top of a Leonida communications tower (about a 7-minute climb) is a nest with colorful eggs. Throwing one triggers a cutscene with a driver. It appears to nod to a similar GTA: Vice City (2002) egg — [GamingBible, 2026-11-20](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-778903-20261120).
- **Dexter Morgan's apartment lookalike (LIKELY):** A Vice City building resembles Dexter's apartment. It cannot be entered — [GamingBible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-778903-20261120).
- **Teleporting, invincible NPC at night (RUMOR):** After you aim a sniper rifle at it, the NPC seemingly becomes invincible and teleports behind the player. It may be a glitch. Reddit-sourced — [GamingBible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-easter-eggs-778903-20261120).
- **Space debris / WAiNK and Inflatable Flamingo (LIKELY, collectible hints):** VICE's cheat-unlock list implies hidden collectible sets: scanning all space debris with WAiNK, 50 "cocaine flamingos", 24 Vice City Flamingos cards, 16 "Girlypop" vehicles, watches, and discoverable vehicles such as Martha's Vamos and the Ridgeway — [VICE, 2026-11-19](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-cheat-codes-list/).
- **Hidden loot locations (LIKELY):** Three Sheets Liquor back room (reached via Shirley's errand); the South Vice-Dale County warehouse/barn NE of Hamlet near Lucia's former prison, with no enemies, weapons, cash, cocaine, and a $6,000 baseball card in a side office — [Insider Gaming, 2026-11-19](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-early-game-locations-make-money/).

### Inferences
- GamingBible places the horse monument "near Manifest Canyon" and also calls it the "Monument of Leonidan Supremacy north of Port Gellhorn". These are probably the same statue. Map pin precision is unverified.
- Expect the community mystery hunt to center on the monument symbols plus the eye/conspiracy truck. The app should keep these in a "Theories" sub-tab.

## Money methods (early/mid/late game, $/hr, stocks/property/business, heists, glitches)

### Takeaway
Money in GTA VI at launch comes from crime: store robberies, fencing loot, car theft for Moses, jewelry/ATM jobs, bank vaults and story heists. No reliable source confirms a GTA V-style stock market or purchasable passive-income businesses. The main exploit, a respawning loot barn near Hamlet, is unpatched as of 2026-11-22.

Using a cheat disables saving and trophies/achievements for that session. Only ~18 sequences are known so far.

### Takeaway

Unpatched — [Dexerto, Michael Gwilliam, 2026-11-19](https://dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-glitch-lets-players-access-first-person-mode-3430025); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-glitch-adds-the-first-person-mode-that-rockstar-skipped/) (body unreadable).
